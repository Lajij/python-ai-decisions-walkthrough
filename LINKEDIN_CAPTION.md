Most of the back office at the banks and insurers I work with runs on Python.

Claims intake. Fraud checks. Ops automation.

Those teams want AI to make decisions inside those systems: what kind of claim is this, how urgent is it, does a person need to see it. Instead they get a JavaScript-only SDK, a vendor per model, and a paragraph of prose someone has to parse with regex.

With Vercel's AI SDK for Python, it's `uv add ai`, one typed question, and a typed answer with a confidence score the code can branch on. Confident claims route themselves. Uncertain ones go to a person. All through one AI Gateway: one key, model choice as config, spend you can see.

In the video I walk through a fictional insurer triaging claim notes this way (example data, simulated walkthrough).

AI decisions, inside the Python you already run.
